Greenhouse automation powered by Priva

As the official Priva partner in Mexico, we install and support climate control, irrigation, monitoring and labor management systems for greenhouses of any scale, nationwide.

Official Priva Partner

Four Priva solutions for your greenhouse

Everything installs and runs on existing greenhouse structures, integrated into a single control platform.

01

Precision climate control

Priva climate controllers regulate temperature, humidity, CO₂ and ventilation in real time, creating the optimal environment for every crop while reducing the greenhouse's energy consumption.

  • Automatic temperature and humidity regulation
  • Ventilation, screen and heating control
  • CO₂ usage optimization

02

Precision irrigation systems

Priva irrigation solutions dose water and fertilizer automatically based on each crop's exact needs, cutting down on water and input waste.

  • Automated fertigation
  • Precise dosing per growing zone
  • Water consumption tracking and analysis

03

Real-time data for better decisions

Greenhouse sensors that capture temperature, humidity, radiation and other key parameters, available from any device so you can make informed decisions instantly.

  • Climate, substrate and plant sensors
  • Real-time dashboards and alerts
  • Historical data for production analysis

04

Plan and control your operation

Priva tools to plan tasks, track labor and control the full crop cycle, improving your field team's productivity.

  • Task and labor planning
  • Full crop-cycle traceability
  • Reports for better decision-making

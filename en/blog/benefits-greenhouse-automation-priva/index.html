Benefits of greenhouse automation with Priva

Greenhouse automation is no longer a luxury reserved for large-scale operations. With solutions like Priva's, Mexican growers of any size can access the precision climate control, irrigation and monitoring that used to be available only in Europe.

1. More precise climate control, less variability

Priva climate controllers adjust temperature, humidity and ventilation in real time, minimizing the variability that affects crop quality and yield. Instead of reacting to weather changes, the system anticipates them.

2. Lower water and energy consumption

Automated irrigation doses water and fertilizer based on the crop's actual needs, avoiding over-watering. Combined with efficient climate control, this translates into measurable savings on water and energy bills.

3. Data to decide, not just to observe

Greenhouse sensors continuously capture climate, substrate and plant data. With this information, growers can spot trends, anticipate problems and adjust their crop strategy based on evidence, not just experience.

4. Better use of labor

Priva's labor management tools let you plan tasks and track team productivity, helping staff focus on the work that has the biggest impact on the crop.

Where to start

You don't need to automate the whole greenhouse at once. At INREPSA we help our clients prioritize the investments with the best return -usually climate control and irrigation- and scale automation gradually.

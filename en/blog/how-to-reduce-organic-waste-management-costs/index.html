How to reduce organic waste management costs

For hotels, restaurants, supermarkets and institutional cafeterias, organic waste collection is often one of the operating costs that grows the most over time -driven by waste volume, distance to the landfill, and collection fees.

1. Measure before you act

The first step is understanding how many kilos of organic waste your operation generates per day. Without that number, it's impossible to correctly size a solution or calculate potential savings.

2. Separate at the source

Separating organic waste from other waste streams right at the point of generation (kitchen, dining area, production floor) is the foundation of any cost-reduction strategy, and it makes any downstream treatment easier.

3. Treat waste on-site

This is where on-site industrial composting makes the biggest difference. A composter like those from Iruña Composting can reduce organic waste volume by up to 80-90% in just a few days, producing a stable pre-compost. That means fewer collection trips and lower tonnage fees.

4. Calculate the return on investment

Comparing your current organic waste collection cost against the cost of operating an on-site composter, most medium and high-volume businesses find an attractive return on investment within a reasonable timeframe.

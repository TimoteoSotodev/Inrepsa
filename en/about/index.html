About us

European technology, solutions built for Mexico

INREPSA is a Mexican company based in Huimilpan, Querétaro, dedicated to bringing high-end greenhouse automation technology and industrial composting solutions to the whole country.

Our story

We were founded to bring Mexican growers, hotels, restaurants and municipalities the technology that is already standard in Europe: automated greenhouses with precision climate control, irrigation and monitoring, and composting systems that solve the organic waste problem right where it's generated.

Today we are the official Priva partner, the Dutch world leader in greenhouse automation, and we represent Iruña Composting in Mexico, part of Grupo Iruña of Spain, with over 50 years of experience and ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certifications.

Our mission

Help our clients produce more efficiently and reduce their organic waste volume by up to 80-90%, through proven technology, professional installation and technical support throughout the process.

Coverage

Headquartered in Querétaro, we serve clients across all of Mexico, from large-scale agricultural projects to hotel chains, supermarkets and municipal governments.

Partnerships

We represent industry-leading brands

Priva

Dutch company, a global reference in automation, climate control, irrigation and monitoring for greenhouses and high-tech growing facilities.

Iruña Composting

Part of Grupo Iruña (Spain), with over 50 years of experience and ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certifications, manufacturer of industrial electromechanical pre-composters.
